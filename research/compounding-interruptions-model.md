# Compounding-interruptions model — 2026-08-05

Companion note to `compounding-interruptions.html` (interactive: two charts, hover, table views).
Built in Cowork session 2026-08-05, prompted by Jude: "model the not-interrupting is the hard part."

## Model setup
- Contribution ladder from the wealth plan (ISA + pension, nominal): £17.1k @22 → £15.9k @23 → £18.5k @24 (£52k salary) → £28k @25–26 (£65k) → £40.4k @27–28 (£80k) → £50k @29 (~£100k TC), then +3%/yr.
- Start pot £14k (Sept-2026 post-move trough). Market: +12%/yr with −25% crashes at ages 26/34/42/50 (≈7.5%/yr nominal CAGR, ≈4.4% real). **All outputs deflated 3%/yr to today's money.**
- Sanity check: unbroken line ≈ £311k @30 — matches the plan's ~£325k base case.

## Chart 1 — behaviour (same ladder, same market)
| Behaviour | @60 (today's £) | Cost vs unbroken |
|---|---|---|
| Unbroken (the plan) | £3.97m | — |
| Panic pauser (stops 3yrs after each crash) | £2.48m | **−£1.49m** |
| Lifestyle creep (raises never captured) | £1.93m | **−£2.04m** |
| House raid @30 (−£80k + 3yrs half-rate) | £3.52m | −£440k (becomes home equity — true cost smaller) |
| Kids downshift (half-rate 33–39) | £3.51m | −£450k |

**Lesson: life events are cheap; behavioural errors are catastrophic.** Panic-pausing skips exactly the cheapest purchases. Crashes themselves cost the unbroken line nothing by 60. Automation over willpower, quantified: DCA surviving crashes ≈ £1.5m; routing every raise into the orders ≈ £2m.

## Chart 2 — career path after 30 (behaviour held perfect)
| Path | @60 |
|---|---|
| Flat real contributions from 29 (base) | £3.97m |
| Steady climb +3% real/yr | £5.51m |
| US-comp jump (real contributions 2× from 32) | £6.64m |
| Coast — stop saving entirely at 40 | £2.33m |
| Max ISA from 22 (needs Synergaise bridge £12–13k/yr at 22–26) | £4.18m (+£58k @30, +£212k @60; only +£50k extra contributed → 4.2× multiplier) |

**Crossover ≈ age 40 (~£1m pot): the pot's own growth overtakes annual contributions.** Pre-40 income trajectory sets the ceiling (worth up to +£2.7m); post-45 raises barely move the age-60 outcome. Coast case shows early money does the structural work.

## Combined conclusion (Jude accepted 2026-08-05)
Savings side is finished business — automation protects the downside. The open variable is income 24→40 (Synergaise / comp trajectory). Early ISA-max is worth doing only if Synergaise funds it without displacing Synergaise-building itself.

## Caveats
Illustrative, not a forecast: crashes don't run on timetables; sequence risk can be worse; ladder assumes ~£100k TC by 29; 7%/3% nominal/inflation assumptions per the wealth plan.
