## Flags before you go

1. **⚠️ The scale.** Every number above assumes weighed portions and diet.md has it as an
   UNVERIFIED open item since 20 Aug — you thought a Salter 1103 was already ordered but confirmed
   the same day you don't have one. If it isn't in the flat, buy one today (~£10–14, Argos/
   Sainsbury's). Without it this list is a guess wearing a table.
2. **Batch-cooking 7 dinners on a Monday is a food-safety problem, not a macro one.** Cooked
   chicken keeps 3–4 days in the fridge, not 7. Fridge days 1–3, freeze days 4–7 on cook night.
   Same for the wraps — assemble 3, freeze the turkey mix for the rest.
3. **No bread this week.** Sourdough kit ETA ~27 Aug, so first bake is week of 31 Aug. Potatoes
   carry the whole carb rail until then — which is fine, they're the better half of it anyway.
4. **Avocado ripeness is the single most likely way this week fails.** 7 avocados bought ripe on
   Monday are brown guacamole by Thursday. Mixed ripeness, or buy 4 now and 3 midweek.
5. **Still open (not changed here): the calorie target.** The 18 Aug energy model put your real
   burn at ~3,075/day, making 2,000 a 900–1,150/day deficit — well past the ~660/day fat-mass
   ceiling, and it widens as mileage climbs. This list is built at the *existing* 2,000 target
   because you haven't ruled on the flat-2,300 vs cycled option yet. Worth deciding before three
   weeks of weigh-ins go in the books against a target you may not want.
