### The one consequence, and it's yours to accept or refuse

L0 needs **two more sessions than it has**. Everything below shifts by two days, and the cheapest
place to absorb it is the top: **L4 (31 Aug–2 Sept) loses two of its three sessions.**

That is the least damaging cut available, and the plan already says so — "realistic by 2 Sept: L0
clean, L1 clean, L2 done, L3 done, **L4 started**", with L4–L5 continuing Sept–Dec at ~3 h/wk, and
Maths L5 being the Stanford gate for January rather than a pre-start target. L4 was always the
overflow.

**Alternatives if you'd rather not:** compress L1 stats (six sessions for material where the one
tested topic came back R, so possibly overfunded), or hold the checkpoint at 9 Aug and let logs go
in underdone. I'd take the L4 slip.

## SQL — the sequence is fine, only the mode changes

Worth saying plainly: the SQL column doesn't need re-cutting. It already climbs L0→L5 in the right
order with checkpoints in the right places. What changes is that each rung gets **taught in the sheet
before it gets repped in the half-hour**, instead of being repped on the assumption the method is
already there.

The one hard data point so far: **`GROUP BY` with `HAVING` was not available on 30 Jul.** That's the
4–6 Aug block, which currently reads as drilling. It needs teaching first.

Everything else waits on Saturday's probe. Rungs 1–5, from the bottom, tagged — and an R has to mean
*attempted and reconstructed*, not *looked familiar*.
