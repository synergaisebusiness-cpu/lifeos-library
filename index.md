# LifeOS Research Library

Agent-owned (except `braindumps/`, which is Jude-originated via the Cowork cockpit). The market-briefing agent writes here twice weekly + on earnings days; jensen-watch scans daily and writes only when there's something new.

- `briefings/` — full dated briefings (snapshots)
- `sectors/` — running per-sector logs (evolution over time)
- `voices/` — what tracked founders & thinkers said, dated (`jensen-huang.md` also fed daily by jensen-watch)
- `themes/` — the four exponential convergence pillars
- `braindumps/` — Jude's brain-dump library: dated captures of his own thinking (learn once, keep forever)
- `company-health/` — fundamentals-only /100 health scores for current holdings (weekday re-scorer on earnings)
- `exponential-watchlist/` — non-holdings that fit the exponential thesis AND clear the company-health fundamentals bar; daily maintainer agent (discovery + re-scores)

Holdings source of truth: `../memory/portfolio-register.md`. Informational only — not advice.
