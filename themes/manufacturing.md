## Log

### 2026-07-26
Japan's ¥80 trillion national robotics push (targeting 10 million AI robots by 2040, Toyota/Fujitsu/Kawasaki/FANUC now in Nvidia's Cosmos Coalition) is a bigger, more concrete industrial-policy commitment than anything comparable in the US. Combined with Boston Dynamics' simplified new Atlas (production-ready, ~30,000 units/yr target) and Figure's BMW pilot (Figure 03, ~1 robot/hour), July 2026 looks like the moment humanoid robotics started producing real if small unit volumes rather than demos. Musk's own admission that Optimus's ramp will be "long and flat" given a near-total lack of a mature component supply chain (~10,000 entirely new Gen3 parts) is a useful reality check — that supply chain (actuators, sensors, specialized components) is itself a research candidate as a picks-and-shovels angle, separate from the robot-makers themselves.

### 2026-07-30
Hyundai's Boston Dynamics acquisition closed Jul 20 alongside a live ~35,000-worker strike at Ulsan demanding guarantees Atlas won't enter Korean production floors without union consent — a genuinely new wrinkle: humanoid-robotics adoption may face organized labor resistance well before it faces technical or cost barriers in some markets, a different risk profile than the "long and flat ramp" supply-chain story Musk has described for Optimus. Both are worth tracking as distinct constraints on the same thesis. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321150/20260721/hyundai-finalizes-boston-dynamics-takeover-workers-strike-over-atlas-same-day.htm)

### 2026-08-03
Ambition and competition both escalated this window: Tesla AI chief Ashok Elluswamy confirmed a 10-million-Optimus-units/year target out of Gigafactory Texas (~Jul 31) — well beyond Musk's own "long and flat ramp" caution from Jul 25 — while BYD confirmed (Jul 27-28) it will unveil its first humanoid robot in early August, the first major Chinese automaker beyond Unitree/AgiBot to commit to a specific public debut date. Worth researching how credible BYD's entry is relative to the more established field (Boston Dynamics/Hyundai, Figure, Unitree, AgiBot) — actual volume production remains pre-scale for every player regardless of messaging.
