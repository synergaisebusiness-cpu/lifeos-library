### 2026-07-26
The grid, not capital or chips, is emerging as the AI buildout's binding constraint — 14-year interconnection queues in Northern Virginia, ~half of 2026's planned data centers at risk of delay. This is the through-line connecting Constellation's first US SMR investment (Blue Energy), GE Vernova's record $176bn backlog, and the Trump administration's Saudi nuclear deal (now in doubt after a last-minute condition) plus its $200m SMR-acceleration initiative. Research candidates worth tracking: SMR developers and gas-turbine/switchgear suppliers beyond names already held, given how tight turbine and grid-equipment capacity has become. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/07/07/ai-data-centers-demand-expansion/)

### 2026-07-30
PJM's Jul 28 decision to formally plan curtailment of large (50MW+) data centers from mid-2027 is the clearest evidence yet the grid bottleneck is becoming operational reality, not just a queueing statistic. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/28/data-centers-may-face-temporary-power-cuts-to-prevent-blackouts-on-largest-us-grid/). New this week: Baird's downgrade of Caterpillar (citing local/political opposition to AI data-center buildouts, e.g. New York's moratorium) shows the market starting to price backlash against buildouts as a distinct risk, separate from raw grid capacity — a two-sided read on the same bottleneck theme. The US-Saudi 123 agreement's confirmation (live 90-day Congressional review clock) and the Unity microreactor's criticality milestone at Idaho National Lab are concrete steps forward for the nuclear/SMR leg, worth tracking through Constellation's Aug 6 print.

### 2026-08-10
Two new fusion/nuclear-fuel data points. Oak Ridge National Laboratory and Rutherford Energy Ventures launched "FULCRA" (Fusion Upscaled Leveraged Consortia for Rapid Acceleration, Aug 7) — a public-private partnership to build shared fusion testbed infrastructure aimed at de-risking critical pilot-plant systems (plasma-facing components, breeding blankets, neutron sources) ahead of a mid-2030s commercialization target, co-founded by MIT's Dennis Whyte and Andrew Lo. [ANS Nuclear Newswire](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-08-07/article-8276/ornl-rutherford-energy-ventures-launch-fusion-partnership/). Separately, Centrus Energy signed an LEU/HALEU supply contract with X-energy for its Xe-100 small modular reactors, expanding Centrus's enrichment backlog to $3bn with deliveries beginning 2030 — a research candidate on the nuclear-fuel supply chain, not a held name. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/centrus-energy-signs-leu-and-haleu-supply-agreement-with-x-energy-302844906.html)

### 2026-08-13
Two genuinely new dated items. Fuse Energy Technologies reported a record fusion neutron yield of 1.27×10¹² in a single shot (Aug 11) — claimed as the highest publicly documented yield in that range by any private/commercial fusion company; a technical milestone, not a commercial one yet. [PowerMag](https://www.powermag.com/fuse-touts-highest-neutron-yield-by-any-fusion-company/). [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/12/heatwave-and-jellyfish-a-fifth-of-french-nuclear-capacity-offline).
