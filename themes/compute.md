## Log

### 2026-07-26
Every major compute buyer is now hedging against single-vendor dependence: Nvidia's $500bn+ SK Group LOI and Alphabet's reported "Frozen v2" custom TPU effort (targeting 6-10x tokens/watt by 2028) both point the same direction, alongside Meta's in-house "Iris" chips and Amazon's own silicon. Counter-signal worth researching: Azeem Azhar flagged (Jul 6 newsletter) that over 95% of Nvidia Grace-Blackwell GPUs remain undeployed despite shipping since Dec 2024 — a shipped-vs-deployed gap that would matter to any near-term chip-demand model. [Exponential View](https://www.exponentialview.co/p/data-to-start-your-week-6-july-2026)

### 2026-07-30
The China DUV lithography story (reports of a Shanghai-backed firm mass-producing immersion DUV tools for SMIC/Hua Hong/CXMT) is the most important compute-pillar development this week — first concrete evidence, not rumor, that China's chip-equipment self-sufficiency timeline may be compressing faster than priced. Worth researching: how close to leading-edge this capacity really is, since immersion DUV still trails ASML's EUV tools by a generation. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/asml-slides-after-report-of-china-beginning-duv-tool-production). On the efficiency side, Azeem Azhar's coverage of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 (2.8T-parameter open MoE model) is a live example of the "compute efficiency frontier" counter-narrative to pure capex-scaling — the same tension visible in Alphabet's "Frozen v2" TPU effort and AMD/Micron's proprietary-supply-lock strategies noted previously.

Worth keeping separate, per Azeem Azhar's framing: this is a leverage/position-sizing failure, not evidence the underlying AGI-compute thesis is wrong. [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/leopold-aschenbrenner-situational-awareness-collapse-crypto/), [Exponential View](https://www.exponentialview.co/p/ev-595). ### 2026-08-13
The clearest, most structurally significant compute-pillar item in weeks: Nvidia's $500bn Wall Street compute-financing consortium (MOUs with Apollo/BlackRock/Blackstone/Brookfield/Goldman Sachs/KKR, announced Aug 10 — full detail in voices/jensen-huang.md) treats GPU compute capacity itself as loan collateral, financed the way real estate or toll roads are. Separately, Energy Vault announced a 1.25GW integrated power-infrastructure deal (with Caterpillar gensets) for an unnamed hyperscaler's AI data center — another data point that power, not chips, is the binding constraint on buildout.
