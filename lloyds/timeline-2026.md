### Bootcamp week 1 in detail (slide marked "representative, subject to change")
- **Mon 21 Sept AM** — intro to product-led AI development, the product life cycle, SDLC,

- **Tue 22 Sept AM** — 🔴 **intermediate SQL, advanced SQL.** *The real pre-start deadline. Not
  3 Sept.*
- **Tue PM** — relational databases, optimising queries

**Where to lead and where not to.** Weeks 2–3 are classical ML, ground the pre-start plan never
covered — be a good teammate there and let others lead. Weeks 5–6 are where being visibly ahead is
earned and welcome. Trying to lead all eight weeks reads badly and is exhausting.

## Standing facts that frame all of the above

**The scheme.** Two years, **three × 8-month placements**. Qualification: **the Artificial
Intelligence Professional Program via Stanford University** — this closes the KB's open question
about what "the Qualification" in the offer letter refers to; it is Stanford, stated on the
DS&AI scheme slide. Placement job families: Data & AI Data Scientist · **Machine Learning & AI
Engineering** · Data Engineering.

**Getting there.** From Elephant & Castle the station is **Moorgate**, not Bank. Both are on the
Northern line's Bank branch; Moorgate is one stop further (E&C → Borough → London Bridge → Bank →
Moorgate) but the walk is **6 minutes vs 10**. The pack's full list: Moorgate 6 min · Liverpool St
(Moorgate exit) 6 min · Barbican 7 min · Bank 10 min · Mansion House 10 min.

**Working pattern.** At least **2 days per week** in your assigned office (the NGT summary words the
same rule as **40% minimum**; any LBG office counts), possibly more depending on role. **Core hours
9–5** 🟡 *confirm with Sean — schedule.md currently assumes 08:00–16:00*. Office travel is in your
own time and at your own expense. Desks booked via **Condeco**. Dress **smart casual**. Anchor days
are explicitly a line-manager conversation — asked of Sean 1 Sept, awaiting reply.

**Contract dates worth having in view.** £5,000 settling-in payment lands the month after starting,
with a **12-month clawback cliff**. Qualification funding runs a **24-month taper**. Mandatory
Training every quarter. **Clause 15 written consent for Synergaise is a week-one action** — it needs
Sean's written consent, specific about scope, and "will not be unreasonably refused".

**Blueprint** — the NGT development programme proper — launches **Q1 2027**, one mandatory
personal-skills course per quarter for graduates plus an optional leadership workshop capped at
150 places. Q4 2026 is "Laying Out the Groundwork", i.e. day 1/2 and AI Basecamp.
