## Hybrid working, hours, practicalities

- **At least 2 days per week in your assigned office**, "however you may be expected to be in the
  office more dependent on your role" (slide 18). The NGT summary phrases the same rule as a
  **40% minimum attendance**; attendance at *any* LBG office counts toward it.
- **Anchor days and team attendance patterns are explicitly a line-manager conversation** — the deck
  says so twice. Confirms this was the right question to put to Sean.
- **Core hours 9–5**, subject to role and line manager.
- **Travel to the office is in your own time and at your own expense.**
- **Desk booking via Condeco** — book and check in on arrival, cancel if not needed.
- **Dress code: smart casual.** ("Dress for your day"; get a feel once in role, discuss with LM.)
- Cameras on for Teams calls is the stated norm.
- Homeworking equipment (desk, chair, monitors) requestable after onboarding, subject to approval.

## Learning programmes

**AI Basecamp — 18 Sept to 6 Nov, weekly Friday-morning sessions.** Eight weeks, learning from AI
leaders, cross-functional. Mandatory for graduates, strongly recommended for apprentices, sessions
recorded. **BUT: the NGT summary states data graduates do the specialist bootcamp instead and are
NOT required to attend AI Base Camp.** ⚠️ Worth confirming, because AI Basecamp's Friday mornings
would otherwise collide directly with the Cambridge Spark bootcamp's Friday in-office day.

**Blueprint — launches Q1 2027.** Refreshed NGT learning programme on quarterly themes:
Q1 *Digging under your surface* (self-awareness) · Q2 *Doing more with AI* · Q3 *Staying ahead of
change* · Q4 *Owning every conversation* (communication). Per quarter: a **mandatory personal-skills
course (graduates only)**, an **optional leadership workshop, 150 spaces**, plus speaker series,
learning playlist and "practical impact" courses open to all NGT with limited spaces.
**Q4 2026 is "Laying Out the Groundwork"** — i.e. day 1/2 + AI Basecamp are that quarter's content.

**Groupwide mandatory training** — required for all new colleagues, plus role-specific training the
line manager assigns.

## Support routes

Line manager is the primary day-to-day contact. Next Gen Talent team for programme concerns.
Recruitment team for vetting, tenancy references and start-date questions. Where a line manager sits
in a different location, local support networks exist at the home hub.
