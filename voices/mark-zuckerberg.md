[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/29/zuckerberg-says-metas-enterprise-ai-opportunity-extends-beyond-agents/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/zuckerberg-metas-ai-capacity-dilemma-what-to-sell-vs-what-to-keep.html).
