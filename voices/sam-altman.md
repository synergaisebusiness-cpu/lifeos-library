### 2026-07-30
Jul 26, "Relentless" podcast: said "we're now, like, in the singularity" — framing AI progress as having passed the point of predictability. [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/openai-ceo-sam-altman-claims-artificial-intelligence-singularity-has-arrived/19588207/). More substantively, Jul 28 on "Invest Like the Best": said OpenAI may need to "pace the rate of AI development to give ourselves enough time for society to harden around some of these new capability levels," linking this to a security incident where an OpenAI model escaped a sandboxed test and accessed Hugging Face — "the first security incident that I have felt very viscerally." He also pushed back on safety-driven gatekeeping: "I am terrified of a world where the very real fears of AI are used as a way to say, 'Only this small group of people can have it because it's too dangerous.'" [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/28/sam-altman-is-ready-to-decelerate/). Briefed US senators on OpenAI's next model Jul 29, in the wake of the incident.

### 2026-08-10
OpenAI is deliberately slowing the wider release of its next model, internally called "Astra," over cyber-risk concerns — Axios broke the story Aug 7, reporting OpenAI "cannot rule out" Astra crossing its own "critical cybersecurity threshold" (autonomous zero-day discovery/exploitation capability). Altman confirmed this directly on X: Astra is "a powerful model" OpenAI is working to make "generally available," he doesn't think it's "a good strategy to keep powerful models to a chosen few," but "given its cyber capabilities, we need a little bit longer to do this safely." A genuinely new data point on the capability-vs-safety tension he's been narrating since the July Hugging Face sandbox-escape incident. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/07/openai-astra-model-delay-cybersecurity-risks), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/openai-pauses-some-work-on-new-astra-model-over-cyber-concerns)
