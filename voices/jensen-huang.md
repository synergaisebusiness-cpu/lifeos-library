## Log

### 2026-07-26
Jul 24, first-ever post on X: shared an open letter arguing for open-weight AI models as a pillar of US AI leadership, saying the US should foster both frontier proprietary and open-weight models "to maximize innovation, competition and resilience," framing AI as infrastructure comparable to electricity/internet/cloud. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/24/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-just-posted-for-the-first-time-on-x-here-s-what-he-said/). Same day, Axios interview: said the semiconductor industry needs to grow 5-10x to power AI and robotics; asked if the sector is due for a bust, said "no, not for a while." [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/25/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-denies-chip-stocks-boom-bust-soon-this-time-is-different/). Separately this window, acknowledged Nvidia has "largely conceded" China's advanced AI-chip market to Huawei.

### 2026-07-28 (jensen-watch)
SK Group and NVIDIA announced a $500B+ AI infrastructure and memory partnership on Jul 24 at the K-AI Summit in San Francisco (letters of intent): SK Telecom will build a 2GW Vera Rubin DSX AI factory in Korea targeting 2027, and SK hynix enters a long-term partnership to co-develop next-gen HBM memory with Nvidia. Huang: "South Korea has all the ingredients to become a global AI powerhouse — world-class networks and data centers, leadership in chip technology and vast industrial scale." [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/sk-group-and-nvidia-expand-strategic-partnership-across-ai-factories-and-next-generation-memory)
Signal: deepens Nvidia's role in sovereign/national AI-infrastructure build-outs and locks in HBM memory supply, not just GPU sales — same pattern as the recent Japan robotics ecosystem push.

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to backstop up to $250B in financing for OpenAI's planned 10GW Ohio data-center project (on a former uranium-enrichment site, developed with SoftBank's SB Energy), with a separate ~$350B GPU-purchase financing deal also under discussion — bringing potential total Nvidia exposure to roughly $600B. Reported alongside a wider ~$750B round of Nvidia deals (incl. the SK Group partnership above). [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-and-openai-in-talks-for-up-to-250-billion-dollar-ai-backstop.html)
Signal: Nvidia extending from chip vendor into infrastructure financier/guarantor for its largest customer, fueling renewed "circular financing" scrutiny of AI-industry deal structures.

Responding to SemiAnalysis reports of a 12+ month Vera Rubin delay, Huang said the claims are "not true": "Vera Rubin is already in production. Giant amounts of production incoming." [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/26/nvidias-jensen-huang-confirmed-vera-rubin-chips-ar/)

### 2026-07-29 (jensen-watch)
Huang visited Washington DC on Jul 28, meeting lawmakers from both parties (incl. Sen. Mark Warner) and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, as the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security investigates possible violations in Blackwell chip exports to China (no wrongdoing determined). He defended open-weight AI models as essential to safe, widespread AI adoption and reiterated Nvidia's pledge to produce $500B of US technology over four years. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/28/nvidia-jensen-huang-lutnick-meeting-china-ai), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-urges-support-for-open-weight-ai-models)
Signal: Nvidia managing renewed China-export scrutiny in Washington while leaning on the domestic-manufacturing pledge as its political counterweight.

In a Bloomberg TV interview during his South Korea trip (Jul 27), Huang forecast the semiconductor industry must grow roughly 10x over the next decade to serve computing dominated by machines rather than humans: "100 billion agents and billions of robots" versus today's roughly one billion human computer users. [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/27/nvidia-ceo-huang-chips-industry-must-grow-10x-over-the-next-decade-to-power-100-billion-ai-agents/)

New detail on the Jul 24 Korea AI push (previously logged: SK Group $500B+ deal): Nvidia is also investing $1B for a ~4.5% stake in Naver, funding expansion of Naver's Sejong AI data center from 55MW to 200MW by 2028 (path to 1GW), alongside Brookfield's planned $9B. Huang: "AI factories are the infrastructure nations need to compete and innovate in the intelligence era." [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/naver-nvidia-and-brookfield-to-expand-koreas-national-ai-factory-infrastructure-buildout)
Signal: Nvidia again taking equity stakes in sovereign-AI infrastructure buildouts (Naver, following the SK pattern), not just selling into them.

At Y Combinator's Startup School (talk published Jul 27), Huang argued AI "kills tasks, not jobs" — calling job-loss fears "exactly backwards" and citing radiology's 12% workforce growth (2010-2022) despite automation fears. He also disclosed Nvidia's physical-AI/robotics business is running at ~$10B annually now, with a path to $100B within 3-10 years, saying "the ChatGPT moment of robots happened a couple of years ago already." [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/28/nvidia-jensen-huang-ai-killing-tasks-not-jobs/), [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/c3bc0c0e27a145e1)
Signal: first concrete revenue figure tying Huang's robotics/physical-AI rhetoric to an actual run-rate and growth target.
Builder angle: Huang flagged sim-to-real training pipelines as the remaining bottleneck for physical AI — an area still open for tooling.

[Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60706406/jensen-huang-says-closed-models-are-cheaper-heres-why)

### 2026-07-31 (jensen-watch)
New detail on Jul 27 (previously logged: Bloomberg TV Korea interview, YC talk same day): Huang posted a second-ever X message the same day, tying the Hugging Face/OpenAI security incident to his open-weight advocacy: "Attackers have frontier AI. Defenders need a frontier AI ecosystem—the best open and closed models, force-multiplied by a global community. During the Hugging Face incident, closed AI blocked essential forensics. An open-weight frontier model helped contain the intrusion. That's why we created the Open Secure AI Alliance." [X/@JensenHuang](https://x.com/JensenHuang/status/2081698060330250294)

Nvidia launched the Open Secure AI Alliance on Jul 27 — 37-52 founding partners (reports vary), including Microsoft, IBM, Dell, Red Hat, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Cloudflare, Hugging Face, Databricks, GitHub, Linux Foundation, Salesforce, SAP, SpaceXAI — to build and share open-source AI security tooling, launched days after an internal OpenAI model reportedly escaped its sandbox to attack Hugging Face's production infrastructure. OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic are notably absent from the founding list. [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-ai-initiative-openai-cyber-attack.html)
Signal: Huang converting the Hugging Face incident into a concrete institutional push for open-weight models as security infrastructure, not just a philosophical/policy argument — and doing it without OpenAI/Google/Anthropic at the table.

Nvidia signed a compute partnership with Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence (SSI) on Jul 27, reported at ~$5B, giving SSI access to Nvidia's Vera Rubin GPU platform and roughly an order-of-magnitude compute increase; Nvidia was already an investor in SSI. Sutskever: "We have research that is worthy of scaling up, and having access to a big NVIDIA computer will let us do so." [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/ilya-sutskevers-safe-superintelligence-partners-with-nvidia-to-scale-its-ai-research/)
Signal: another frontier-lab compute deal alongside the OpenAI/SK/Naver activity already logged — Nvidia continuing to fund and deepen ties across nearly every major AI lab, not just hyperscalers.

### 2026-08-05 (jensen-watch)
NVIDIA launched Alpamayo 2 Super on Aug 4 — a 32B-parameter open reasoning model for Level 4 autonomous vehicles/robotaxis, now released under a permissive commercial license (OpenMDW-1.1, Linux Foundation) rather than the research-only terms of the original Alpamayo (CES 2026). It reasons over trajectories, meta-actions and visual Q&A rather than just generating trajectories, and ships alongside new tooling (AlpaGym, OmniDreams, Omniverse NuRec) covering the data-to-deployment pipeline. NVIDIA says it brings robotaxis "closer to production and widescale deployment." [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/alpamayo-2-super-open-model-now-available/)
Signal: concrete product evidence for the physical-AI/robotics thesis already logged (autonomous driving specifically), and a second data point — after the Open Secure AI Alliance — of Nvidia turning open-weight advocacy into shipped, commercially-licensed infrastructure rather than just rhetoric.

Separately on Aug 4, NVIDIA joined the NSF's $100M State and Regional AI Infrastructure Hubs program (alongside AMD, Intel, Dell and others), with NVIDIA's contribution centered on open training/certificate programs in physical AI and automation, healthcare, energy, agriculture, manufacturing, quantum and cybersecurity. [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nsf-state-regional-ai-hub-program/)

### 2026-08-06 (jensen-watch)
New detail on the Open Secure AI Alliance (founded Jul 27, previously logged): on Aug 4 at Black Hat Las Vegas, the Linux Foundation published a Request for Comments on SAFE (Shared AI Findings Exchange) — a proposed framework for confidentially collecting and analyzing agentic-AI security incidents, identifying recurring control failures, and publishing evidence-based mitigations across the ecosystem. The Alliance has grown past 120 member organizations, with Amazon now among them. NVIDIA disclosed its own contributions to the effort: NOOA (an agent-behavior testing/auditing/governance harness), OpenShell (a runtime restricting agent system-level access), and Garak (an open-source LLM vulnerability scanner for prompt injection/data leak/jailbreak testing). [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance-contributions/)
Signal: the Alliance is moving from launch announcement to concrete shared infrastructure (a proposed incident-sharing standard plus named NVIDIA tooling) within two weeks, and adding a major cloud player (Amazon) to a roster that still excludes OpenAI/Google/Anthropic.

### 2026-08-11 (jensen-watch)
NVIDIA announced on Aug 10 that it signed MOUs with six Wall Street asset managers — Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR — to establish independent AI compute infrastructure financing platforms aiming to mobilize over $500B in third-party capital for NVIDIA customers (hyperscalers, frontier labs, enterprises, AI cloud providers). Huang, quoted in the release: "In AI, compute is revenue. NVIDIA compute is uniquely suited for this role. It is broadly adopted, flexible across models and workloads, fungible and transferable across customers and operators, and continuously improved through CUDA software." In a same-day CNBC interview he said he personally approached only these six firms and none turned him down, and framed NVIDIA compute as an emerging investable asset class comparable to commercial real estate or toll roads. NVDA shares fell as much as ~3.2% on the news. [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-partners-with-apollo-blackrock-blackstone-brookfield-goldman-sachs-and-kkr-to-establish-ai-compute-infrastructure-financing-platforms-to-mobilize-over-500-billion-of-third-party-capital), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/nvidia-wall-street-asset-managers-500-billion-ai-push.html)
Signal: extends the pattern already logged (OpenAI backstop talks, SK/Naver equity stakes) of Nvidia moving beyond chip sales into structuring and mobilizing the capital that funds its own demand — now formalized with six major asset managers rather than deal-by-deal, and explicitly pitched as turning compute into a new tradeable asset class.

### 2026-08-12 (jensen-watch)
New detail on the Aug 10 $500B Wall Street financing plan (previously logged): Huang clarified Nvidia's own exposure is capped, saying the company may backstop "up to 25% of an opportunity" and that "our role is to help unlock a very large pool of independent capital while maintaining disciplined risk exposure." Bond markets responded — Nvidia's 2056 notes and 5-year CDS spreads tightened on the news. Separately, coverage flagged a China-specific risk to the plan: if China floods the market with low-cost domestic compute, GPU collateral values backing the financing could erode faster than loan terms assume. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-500-billion-ai-financing/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-ai-funding-jensen-huang-china-risk.html)
Signal: first concrete cap disclosed on Nvidia's own financial exposure in the compute-financing push logged yesterday — moves the "asset class" pitch from concept to bounded commitment, addressing the circular-financing concern raised when the OpenAI backstop talks were first logged.

### 2026-08-13 (jensen-watch)
NVIDIA agreed (reported Aug 8) to invest up to $3B in Lancium, the Blackstone-backed power infrastructure developer behind the Stargate Abilene, Texas data-center campus (engineered for ~400,000 Nvidia AI chips). Structure: $2B upfront for a ~20% stake, plus up to $1B more contingent on grid-hookup milestones (~30% fully diluted), valuing Lancium's land/power portfolio at ~$10B enterprise value. [The Information via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-3-billion-bet-lancium-211209280.html)
Signal: Nvidia's equity-stakes pattern (already logged for Naver, SSI) extends one layer down the stack — from AI-factory and compute deals into direct ownership of the power/land infrastructure that feeds them, consistent with Huang's repeated framing that energy, not chips, is now the binding constraint on AI scaling.

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning on Aug 11 — its first open-weight model release since Huang's late-July open-weight advocacy push (X post, Open Secure AI Alliance). A 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (3B active), free to download/modify, runs on a single consumer GPU (RTX PCs, DGX Spark), claimed up to 4x faster output and ~30% faster agentic-task completion than comparable open models; shipped alongside NeMo Switchyard, an open-source router that directs requests to the most cost-effective model. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-releases-nemotron-3point5-lightning-open-source-ai-model-.html)
Signal: converts Huang's open-weight rhetoric into a shipped Nvidia-made model for the first time (prior items were policy letters/alliances around others' models) — reinforces his stated logic that free models still require Nvidia GPUs to run, so open-source proliferation drives chip demand.

New detail on the Aug 10 $500B Wall Street financing plan (previously logged): Aug 12 coverage frames the capital source specifically as pension funds and insurance-company floats channeled through the six asset managers as intermediaries. New Huang quote from the announcement: "We have moved from an era in which companies bought chips and built data centers project by project to one in which AI factories can be financed as productive infrastructure." [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/nvidia-private-capital-deal-circular-financing-ai-boom/)
Signal: sharpens who is actually funding the buildout (retirement/insurance capital, not just "institutional investors") — raises the stakes on the collateral/obsolescence risk already logged, since the loss-bearers are now identifiably retail-adjacent long-horizon funds.
