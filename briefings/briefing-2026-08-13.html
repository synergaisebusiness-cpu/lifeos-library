Exponential thesis watch

Compute: the clearest dated item is Nvidia's $500bn Wall Street financing consortium (Aug 10) — see the sector section and voices log above for full detail; this is a genuinely new structural pattern (GPU compute treated as loan collateral, financed like real estate or toll roads) worth tracking as it moves from MOU to deployed capital. A smaller, related data point: Energy Vault announced a 1.25GW integrated power-infrastructure deal (with Caterpillar gensets via an EPC partner) for an unnamed hyperscaler's AI data center — a further illustration of power, not chips, as the binding constraint on compute buildout. Research candidate: Energy Vault.

Energy: the richest pillar this window, with two genuinely new dated items. Fuse Energy Technologies reported a record fusion neutron yield of 1.27×10¹² in a single shot (Aug 11) — claimed as the highest publicly documented yield in that range by any private/commercial fusion company; worth researching further as a technical milestone, not a commercial one yet. Separately, ~20% of French nuclear capacity went offline (Aug 12) — 13 of 57 reactors affected by a combination of heatwave, drought, and a jellyfish invasion clogging cooling intakes — a vivid, structural illustration of climate-driven fragility in baseload nuclear generation, relevant background for the SMR/grid-resilience thesis. On the fuel-supply side, the Centrus Energy/X-energy HALEU supply agreement (signed Aug 4-6, just at the window's edge) builds Centrus's enrichment backlog toward $3bn with 2030 deliveries — research candidates: Fuse Energy Technologies, Centrus Energy, X-energy. (PowerMag — Fuse Energy, Euronews — France nuclear)

Manufacturing/robotics: no development landed squarely inside Aug 10-13 for this pillar beyond the Unitree IPO subscription/lottery mechanics already covered in the robotics section above (the clearest standout this window regardless). Google DeepMind's "Gemini Robotics 2" physical-AI model release and a small robotics seed round (Avatar Robotics, $6.5m) both landed just before the window (Aug 4-9) and are flagged as recent context rather than fresh news.

Biology: the thinnest pillar this window. The closest genuinely in-window item is press coverage (Aug 12) of Chai Discovery, a ~2.5-year-old AI protein-design startup positioning drug discovery as "software-like" — a research candidate worth a closer look, alongside the standing AI-native biology cohort (Isomorphic Labs, Insilico Medicine, Xaira Therapeutics, Recursion, EvolutionaryScale) that continues to draw large rounds but, industry-wide, has yet to produce a single FDA-approved AI-discovered drug.
